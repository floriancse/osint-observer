Ukraine and Russia at War

Ukraine, Russia

Ukrainian Drone Barrage Shuts Moscow Airspace for Seven Hours, Stranding 370 Flights

Ukrainian drones forced a near seven-hour airspace shutdown over Moscow, delaying or canceling more than 370 flights, while Mayor Sergei Sobyanin claimed 134 drones were downed.

Ukraine Strikes Gazprom Refinery 2,400 km Inside Russia

Ukrainian deep-strike drones struck the Surgut Oil Refining and Petrochemical Complex in Russia's Khanty-Mansiysk district, approximately 2,400 km from the Ukrainian border, targeting a Gazprom facility that produces gasoline and aviation fuel. The attack represents one of the deepest strikes into Russian territory of the war, underscoring Ukraine's expanding reach against strategic energy infrastructure.

Ukraine's Neptune Strike Destroys Six Russian Su-34s at Borisoglebsk Air Base

Ukrainian Navy cruise missiles destroyed six Russian Su-34M fighter-bombers at Borisoglebsk Air Base in Russia's Voronezh region, with satellite imagery confirming the losses on the night of October 15, 2026. The strike, part of a broader Ukrainian campaign against Russian airpower, also included drone raids on Moscow and a missile attack on the same airfield, marking a significant escalation in Ukraine's long-range strike capabilities.

Israeli forces launched airstrikes, artillery fire, and drone operations on Ali al-Taher Hill in Lebanon's Nabatieh district on August 16, 2026, in direct response to ongoing Hezbollah attacks. This escalation intensifies cross-border hostilities and raises the risk of a broader regional conflict.

The Israeli Air Force struck the town of Al-Mansouri in Lebanon's Tyre District, escalating cross-border hostilities and raising fears of a broader regional conflict.

Mali Airstrikes Kill Eight Civilians as Nigeria Attacks Leave 20 Troops Dead

Nigeria: Air Strike Kills 40 ISWAP Fighters in Borno

The Nigerian military conducted a precision air strike in Borno State, eliminating over 40 ISWAP fighters and destroying their vehicles. This operation marks a significant blow to the insurgent group's operational capacity in the region.

Nigeria: 10 October Airstrike Kills Over 40 ISWAP Fighters in Borno

The Nigerian Army announced that an airstrike on 10 October in Badu Village, Borno State, killed over 40 ISWAP terrorists, including a senior commander, and destroyed two vehicles and about 20 motorcycles.

Pakistani security forces retaliated against unprovoked Afghan Taliban firing in the Kurram sector along the Pakistan-Afghanistan border, inflicting significant casualties with 12 bodies and 11 injured Taliban personnel brought to Shehrnaw Hospital.

Ethiopia-Eritrea Border Clashes Reported Near Bure as ENDF Claimed to Seize Deba Sima

Unverified reports emerged on 12 October 2026 of renewed fighting along the Ethiopia-Eritrea border near Bure, roughly 70 km from the Eritrean port of Assab, with pro-government pages claiming the Ethiopian National Defense Force (ENDF) captured the Eritrean border town of Deba Sima. The claims, which could not be independently confirmed, raise the risk of a fresh interstate conflict between the two former allies and threaten the stability of the Red Sea corridor.
