Yemen Houthi War

Yemen, Saudi Arabia, Houthis

Houthis strike Yanbu pumping station as Yemen fighting escalates

Yemeni Houthi forces targeted Saudi Arabia's pumping station 11 in Yanbu, amid ongoing reciprocal strikes between the Houthis, Saudi Arabia and the internationally recognized government in Aden. Forces of Yemen's Presidential Leadership Council used a Chinese Norinco LG5 automatic grenade launcher, likely supplied by Saudi Arabia, to target Ansar Allah (Houthi) positions in Yemen. Houthi forces struck numerous PLC vehicles and positions with armed drones, including Rajoum hexacopters dropping O-832/Type 53 pattern bombs and improvised munitions, on several fronts in Yemen.

The Saudi-led Coalition released footage of Royal Saudi Air Force strikes destroying a ballistic missile component factory and a solid rocket propellant chemical plant belonging to Ansar Allah (Houthis) in Sanaa, Yemen. On the Taiz front, Ansar Allah fighters carried out several sniper attacks using designated marksman and anti-materiel sniper rifles. In Marib Governorate, Ansar Allah fighters were seen driving in a convoy carrying a US-made M4A1 carbine with a UID tag and a Type 80 machine gun.

Houthi forces attacked the Aramco Tower in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, causing damage. In response, the Saudi-led Coalition's Royal Saudi Air Force conducted airstrikes destroying Ansar Allah (Houthi) ballistic missile launchers in Saada and Sanaa governorates, Yemen. Separately, Yemeni government forces shot down a Ukrainian Shark drone and FPV drones near Al-Sabiha, about 10 drones in the last 72 hours.
