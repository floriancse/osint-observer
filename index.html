Iran-US War

Houthi Missile Strike on Riyadh Airport Kills Twelve and Injures Over 300

A ballistic missile strike on Terminal 4 at King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh killed twelve people and injured more than 300, according to the Saudi-led coalition, which also reported an earlier Ansarullah attack on Terminal 3 the previous day.

Houthi Strike on Riyadh Airport Kills 12, Halts Air Traffic

Houthi forces struck King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, on October 11, 2026, killing at least 12 people and injuring 50, according to multiple officials cited by The New York Times. The attack halted air traffic, forced evacuations, and triggered a THAAD interceptor launch near the airport as hospitals were overwhelmed with dozens of injured, mostly Saudi nationals.

Houthi Drone Strike Ignites Fire at Aramco Depot in Jeddah

On October 5, a Houthi drone strike caused a fire at the Aramco storage depot in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, marking a significant escalation in the conflict. The attack follows a Houthi missile strike that killed a Saudi pilot at King Khalid International Airport and prompted Saudi airstrikes on Sanaa.

Ukraine and Russia at War

Ukraine, Russia

Russia's Oct 10 Strike on Zaporizhzhia Kills 20 Including Four Children

A Russian airstrike on a residential building in Zaporizhzhia on October 10 killed 20 people, including four children, and injured 17 others, with search and rescue operations now concluded. The attack occurred as Ukrainian drones struck a Yandex data center in Vladimir, Russia, disrupting services, and Russian forces bombed a supermarket in Kharkiv, killing three civilians.

Russia Kills 12 in Zaporizhzhia Bombing, Including Three Children

Russian forces dropped five guided bombs on homes in Zaporizhzhia, killing 12 people including three children and wounding 14, according to Zelensky. The attack is part of a series of strikes on the city that have caused civilian casualties.

Ukraine Drones Hit LUKOIL's Ukhta Refinery Deep Inside Russia

Ukrainian drones struck the Ukhta oil refinery in Russia's Komi Republic, penetrating more than 1,700 km of Russian airspace, while separate strikes hit the Omsk refinery and a Yandex data center in Kaluga, according to President Zelensky. The Ukhta facility, part of LUKOIL and capable of processing about 4.2 million tonnes of crude annually, was set ablaze, marking one of the deepest Ukrainian strikes into Russian territory.

Conflicts in Ethiopia

Ethiopia, Eritrea

Ethiopia-Eritrea Border Clashes Reported Near Bure as ENDF Claimed to Seize Deba Sima

Unverified reports emerged on 12 October 2026 of renewed fighting along the Ethiopia-Eritrea border near Bure, roughly 70 km from the Eritrean port of Assab, with pro-government pages claiming the Ethiopian National Defense Force (ENDF) captured the Eritrean border town of Deba Sima. The claims, which could not be independently confirmed, raise the risk of a fresh interstate conflict between the two former allies and threaten the stability of the Red Sea corridor.

Tigray Peace Force Seizes Shire and Eastern Towns as ENDF Drone Hits Adwa Hotel

The Tigray Peace Force captured the key towns of Shire, Idaga Hamus, Atsbi, and the Weri area in a sweeping offensive across Ethiopia's Tigray Region, while an ENDF drone strike damaged the Shewit International Hotel in Adwa.

Ethiopian Forces Capture Mekelle, Seize Iranian Drone and Foreign Arms

Ethiopian National Defense Force troops captured Mekelle, the capital of the Tigray Region, from the Tigray Defense Forces and filmed weapons they found, including Chinese GB-25 laser-guided munitions, U.S. Mk 82 bombs, Russian FAB-500ShN bombs, R-60M/MK and R-73E air-to-air missiles, and a dismantled Iranian Mohajer-6 UCAV. Numerous drone strikes occurred overnight in Adigrat, Ethiopia, with an Adigrat resident telling AFP that Eritrean trucks were destroyed and Eritrean soldiers fled into the hills north of the city.

Conflicts in Sahel

Mali, JNIM, Nigeria, ISWAP, Burkina Faso, Niger, Chad, Togo, Benin

Mali: FLA Drone Strike Hits Malian and Russian Bases in Soumpi

On October 12, 2026, Azawad Liberation Front (FLA) Tuareg militants used an FPV kamikaze drone armed with a likely PG-7V/T69-1 HEAT RPG warhead to strike Malian Army and Russian Africa Corps bases in Soumpi, Timbuktu Region, Mali, marking a significant escalation in the use of advanced drone tactics against government and Russian forces.

IS-Sahel Attack on Nigerian Base in Kebbi Kills Two on October 7

Suspected IS-Sahel militants attacked a Nigerian army Forward Operating Base in Maiyama, Kebbi State, on October 7, killing two Nigerian security personnel before troops repelled the assault and seized motorcycles and two weapons.

Mali: Army and Africa Corps Retake Kidal After FLA Withdrawal in October 2025

Malian Armed Forces and Africa Corps recaptured Kidal after the Azawad Liberation Front withdrew on October 6, following airstrikes from October 1 to 7.

Sudan

Sudan: RSF Drone Strike on Blue Nile Displacement Camp Kills 30

On October 11, 2026, a drone strike by the UAE-backed Rapid Support Forces (RSF) on Abu Shandi camp in Wad el-Mahi, Blue Nile State, Sudan, killed at least 30 displaced people, marking a severe escalation in the targeting of civilian sites.

Sudan: Army Retakes Al Mazroub as Taasis Drones Strike Al Dilling

The Sudanese army recaptured Al Mazroub in North Kordofan, while drones of the Taasis alliance targeted sites in Al Dilling, Sudan.

Sudan: WFP Convoy Driver Killed in South Kordofan Airstrike as SAF Repels RSF Attack in Blue Nile

An airstrike on a World Food Program convoy in South Kordofan, Sudan, killed a driver, as the Sudanese Armed Forces repelled an RSF and SPLA-N attack on Minza and Bashkarkad in Blue Nile State near the Ethiopian border.

Pakistan-Afghanistan War

Pakistan, Afghanistan

Pakistan Retaliates in Kurram, Afghan Taliban Suffer 12 Dead

Pakistani security forces retaliated against unprovoked Afghan Taliban firing in the Kurram sector along the Pakistan-Afghanistan border, inflicting significant casualties with 12 bodies and 11 injured Taliban personnel brought to Shehrnaw Hospital.

TTP Drone Strike Hits Pakistani Post in South Waziristan

TTP militants used a commercial quadcopter drone to drop an M57D A2 mortar bomb on a Pakistani military post in South Waziristan, marking a notable escalation in the group's tactical capabilities. The attack underscores the growing threat posed by weaponized commercial drones to Pakistani security forces in the region.

NRF Kills Taliban Deputy Intelligence Chief in Badakhshan Attack

The National Resistance Front (NRF) claimed responsibility for an attack in Faizabad, Badakhshan, Afghanistan, that killed Safiullah Motawakel, the Taliban's deputy intelligence chief for Faizabad, along with two bodyguards on Sunday morning.

Myanmar

Myanmar Airstrike Kills Mother and Three Daughters in Sagaing

Myanmar's military killed a mother and her three daughters in an aerial assault on a village in Depayin Township, Sagaing Region, part of an escalating campaign of civilian targeting in resistance strongholds.

KIA Seizes Myanmar Junta Hill Base in Mongmit After Bhamo Outpost Capture

The Kachin Independence Army seized a tactical hill base in Mongmit, Myanmar, days after capturing another regime outpost on its Bhamo battle front, marking a fresh setback for the military junta in northern Shan State.

Myanmar Airstrike on Kyauktaw Market Kills 49, Arakan Army Vows Retaliation

A military airstrike on a market in Kyauktaw, Rakhine State, Myanmar, killed 49 people, marking the deadliest aerial assault in the state since the conflict began nearly three years ago. The Arakan Army vowed retaliation for the attack.

Israel Strikes Nabatieh Hill in Response to Hezbollah Attacks

Israeli forces launched airstrikes, artillery fire, and drone operations on Ali al-Taher Hill in Lebanon's Nabatieh district on August 16, 2026, in direct response to ongoing Hezbollah attacks. This escalation intensifies cross-border hostilities and raises the risk of a broader regional conflict.

Israeli Airstrike Hits Lebanese Town Amid Rising Tensions

The Israeli Air Force struck the town of Al-Mansouri in Lebanon's Tyre District, escalating cross-border hostilities and raising fears of a broader regional conflict.

Israel Strikes Hezbollah Operative in Lebanon Killing Man Behind IDF Officer's Death

The Israel Defense Forces killed a Hezbollah operative in Deir Seryan, Lebanon, in a tank strike and gunfire attack that targeted the individual responsible for the death of IDF officer David Hazutt. This operation underscores the escalating cross-border violence between Israel and Hezbollah along the northern frontier.

US Military Positioned for Potential Attack on Cuba

US military forces are positioned to conduct a potential attack on Cuba contingent upon receiving an order from President Trump, according to Politico reports. This development marks a significant escalation in US-Cuba relations and raises concerns about regional stability.
