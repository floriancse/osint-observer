Ukraine and Russia at War

Ukraine, Russia

Russia's October 13 Ballistic Strike on Kyiv Hits Residential Building and Power Plant, Injuring 13

Russian ballistic missiles struck Kyiv at around 22:40 on October 13, 2026, hitting a high-rise residential building and a combined heat and power plant despite an energy ceasefire, injuring at least 13 people and prompting evacuations due to fire and collapse risk. The attack occurred without launch warning from partners, and Ukrainian President Zelensky warned that Russian intelligence indicates preparations for further ballistic missile launches.

Russian Drone Hits Kyiv Residential Tower, Killing Two

A Russian jet-powered Geran-5 drone struck a 25-story residential building in Kyiv's Seven complex in Osokorky on October 13, 2026, killing two people and injuring at least seven. The attack, part of a broader overnight assault that also hit an apartment building in Kharkiv and a power plant, came as President Zelensky accused Russia of violating a US-brokered energy ceasefire.

Russia's Oct 10 Strike on Zaporizhzhia Kills 20 Including Four Children

A Russian airstrike on a residential building in Zaporizhzhia on October 10 killed 20 people, including four children, and injured 17 others, with search and rescue operations now concluded. The attack occurred as Ukrainian drones struck a Yandex data center in Vladimir, Russia, disrupting services, and Russian forces bombed a supermarket in Kharkiv, killing three civilians.

Ethiopia-Eritrea Border Clashes Reported Near Bure as ENDF Claimed to Seize Deba Sima

Unverified reports emerged on 12 October 2026 of renewed fighting along the Ethiopia-Eritrea border near Bure, roughly 70 km from the Eritrean port of Assab, with pro-government pages claiming the Ethiopian National Defense Force (ENDF) captured the Eritrean border town of Deba Sima. The claims, which could not be independently confirmed, raise the risk of a fresh interstate conflict between the two former allies and threaten the stability of the Red Sea corridor.
